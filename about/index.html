The Founders' Vision

How Mayyattil Waterfront Villa Came to Be

Mayyattil Waterfront Villa was born from a simple but profound dream: to establish an eco-conscious, private sanctuary where families and close friends can escape the high-stress demands of modern life and reconnect at their own natural pace.

Recognizing the unmatched beauty and serenity of the Narakkal backwaters, we set out to create a private estate buyout. This ensures that when you stay with us, there are no other guests, no shared spaces, and no compromises. You receive 100% exclusive access to the villa, the private pool, the lush waterfront grounds, and our warm, dedicated hospitality.

We designed every corner of the villa to showcase Vypin's serene landscapes, from the sunrise balconies of our Master Suite to the gentle breezes rolling off the water onto our sunset deck.

Our Local Roots

The Heritage of Vypin Island & Narakkal

Vypin Island is a spectacular, narrow strip of land bounded by the Arabian Sea to the west and the peaceful backwaters to the east. It is a land deeply steeped in coastal Kerala history, where coconut plantations, traditional coir weaving, and Chinese fishing nets define the landscape.

Narakkal, the peaceful village where Mayyattil stands, is famous for its serene, winding canals and lush aquaculture farms. It represents the authentic, slow-paced charm of rural Kerala that remains largely untouched by mass tourism.

Local Cultural Exploration

Just a 5-minute walk (550 m) to the iconic Boche Toddy Pub, offering a legendary taste of authentic local cuisine and culture.

Beach & Backwater Union

Enjoy the backwaters right at our doorstep, or take a quick 25-minute drive (13 km) to the golden sands of Cherai Beach.
