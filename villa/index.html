One interconnected riverfront home. Five living spaces. Yours alone for the length of the stay.

Master Waterfront Suite

The principal bedroom looks toward the water. A king bed sits under teak finishes, with a private sunrise balcony for tea before the rest of the house wakes. Dressing space is generous; the suite stays fully air-conditioned through Kerala’s warm afternoons.

This is the quietest corner of the villa — still a few steps from the pavilion, never cut off from family life.

King waterfront bed Private sunrise balcony En-suite rain bath Teak dressing suite

Living & Dining Pavilion

The social heart of the house: an air-conditioned lounge that opens onto the waterfront veranda. Evenings collect around the ten-seater dining table; mornings spill onto the veranda with backwater breeze and coffee.

A butler pantry keeps the kitchen close without turning the pavilion into a service corridor. This is where the whole party sits together — meals, games, rain-day films.

Air-conditioned lounge Waterfront veranda 10-seater dining Butler pantry access

Private Pool & Sun Deck

The pool sits on the water’s edge and belongs only to your stay. Teak loungers, filtered water, and a deck aimed at the evening light. No other guests, no shared towels, no resort timetable.

Swim at noon, or wait for the sky to turn over the backwaters. The lawn and pavilion are a few steps away if someone wants shade instead of sun.

100% private pool Cushioned sun loungers Crystal-clear water Sunset deck views

Tropical Ensuite Bath

A stone soaking tub and rainfall shower, set with botanical greenery and organic bath amenities. The ensuite is meant as a pause after the pool or a slow start before breakfast — not a cramped hotel bathroom.

Natural stone and timber keep the room cool to the eye, even when the day outside is bright.

Deep soaking stone tub Open rainfall shower Botanical greenery Natural stone vanity

Riverside Lawn & Sunset Bank

Emerald lawns run to the backwater under coconut canopy. Mornings suit yoga or a quiet walk; late afternoon is high tea on the deck; after dark, a starlit table by the water if you ask the host to set it.

Kayaks and fishing can be arranged from this edge of the estate. The bank is still part of the villa — not a public promenade.

Emerald backwater lawns Sunset high-tea deck Direct waterfront access Evening starlit lounge

How a day on the estate tends to move

Morning

Tea on the master balcony, then breakfast in the pavilion while the lawn is still cool. Optional kayak on the canal before the sun climbs.

Afternoon

Pool and loungers, or the air-conditioned lounge if the heat settles. Children on the lawn; adults in the shade of the veranda.

Evening

A Kerala table for the whole party, then the sunset bank. The house stays yours after dark — no other keys turning in the gate.
