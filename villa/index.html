Master Waterfront Suite

The principal bedroom looks toward the water. A king bed sits under teak finishes, with wall-to-wall sliding glass doors opening to sweeping river views. Dressing space is generous, and the suite stays fully air-conditioned through Kerala’s warm afternoons.

This is the quietest corner of the villa — peaceful, private, and filled with natural sunlight.

Air-Conditioned Living Lounge

The climate-controlled social heart of the house, featuring plush sofa seating, floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the riverfront garden, and a seamless connection to the outdoor veranda.

This is where the whole party gathers together — relaxed conversations, games, and afternoon downtime in air-conditioned comfort.

Air-conditioned lounge Garden & river view Family dining area Ambient lighting

Open-Air Veranda & Sit-Out

A shaded tropical veranda equipped with ceiling fans and comfortable wicker lounge seating. Steps lead directly onto the lush lawns, offering a tranquil spot for morning coffee or afternoon breezes.

Feel the gentle backwater wind while staying protected from the sun under the broad shaded eaves.

Shaded tropical veranda Overhead ceiling fans Wicker sit-out seating Direct garden access

Private Riverside Deck & Water Steps

Dedicated timber steps and a waterfront deck platform built right on the edge of the Narakkal backwaters. Ideal for morning tea, casting a fishing line, or watching the sunset over the quiet water.

How a day on the estate tends to move

Morning

Tea on the master balcony, then breakfast in the pavilion while the lawn is still cool. Optional kayak on the canal before the sun climbs.

Afternoon

Pool and loungers, or the air-conditioned lounge if the heat settles. Children on the lawn; adults in the shade of the veranda.

Evening

A Kerala table for the whole party, then the sunset bank. The house stays yours after dark — no other keys turning in the gate.
