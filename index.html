Our Story & Sanctuary

Where Families Slow Down & Reconnect

Nestled along the peaceful backwaters of Narakkal in Vypin, Mayyattil Waterfront Villa is a modern luxury retreat designed for families and groups seeking a serene escape immersed in nature.

Surrounded by scenic water views and lush tropical greenery, our estate seamlessly blends high-end comfort with the calm, gentle rhythms of coastal Kerala. Waking up to gentle waters, feeling the cool backwater breeze, and enjoying complete whole-villa privacy defines the experience we create for you.
